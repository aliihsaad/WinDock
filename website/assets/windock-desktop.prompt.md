## Final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Create one exceptionally art-directed premium product photograph, wide cinematic 16:9. This image will represent WinDock on a refined software landing page. Show a modern Windows desk with WinDock running on a landscape Android phone. The visual must feel like a carefully composed editorial photograph for a high-end hardware brand, NOT a generic cozy AI workspace. Extremely restrained composition: a matte charcoal desk against a soft cool gray studio wall, one slim frameless widescreen monitor on a simple black stand, one low-profile dark compact keyboard, one small mouse, and ONE black Android phone in a discreet dark landscape stand. Nothing else. No plants, coffee, books, lamps, code editor, writing, clutter or decorations. Camera close to desk height at a subtle front three-quarter angle. The phone is in the near foreground on the right, very prominent, large and tack sharp, roughly 32% of the image width, beside rather than obscuring the keyboard. The entire monitor is visible in the middle background on the left, with a real subtle Windows 11 centered taskbar and a clean abstract midnight blue / muted sage desktop wallpaper, no open windows. The phone displays the WinDock app: a fullscreen dark muted blue-green gradient with exactly six LARGE authentic recognizable app icons arranged in an immaculate 3 columns x 2 rows grid: top row Chrome, Visual Studio Code, Spotify; bottom row Discord, yellow Windows File Explorer folder, Steam. The phone's landscape screen has NO text, NO names under icons, NO status bar, NO titles, NO dashboard panels, NO clock; just the beautiful large icons with generous space and two tiny page dots, a very thin gesture line at the bottom. Correct realistic Android phone proportions and restrained thin black bezel. Balanced asymmetrical composition with generous clean breathing room, precise perspective, subtle graphite texture, realistic reflections, premium subtle edge lighting. Soft broad daylight from left, cinematic yet natural, tasteful cool silver and desaturated sage highlights, deep charcoal shadows retaining detail. Visually coherent, elegant, contemporary and intentional. Real photographic optics, natural depth of field with phone crisp and monitor still readable, no dramatic bokeh. No neon lights or gaming RGB. No Apple devices or logos. No slogan, typography, watermarks or framing graphics.
