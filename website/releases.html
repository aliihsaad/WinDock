LATEST RELEASEOctober 26, 2026

0.2.5

Choose your PC. Pick up where you left off.

PC switching, clipboard access, visible update checks, and a Linux host that stays out of your way.

  • Switch PC: find running Windows and Linux hosts on your local network, choose one inside the Android app, and remember your PCs. Forget stays forgotten after restarting; you can still find and select that PC again.
  • PC clipboard: expand the touchpad panel, choose recent text, edit it, and tap Paste to PC. Browsing alone sends nothing. Windows uses Windows + V history; Linux reads a running clipboard manager, including Omarchy’s own. With no manager, Linux offers the current clipboard.
  • Visible update checks: open Updates from the dock drawer or Appearance, the Android connection screen, or the Windows connection window. Downloads and installation stay manual.
  • Linux in the background: close the connection window and keep using the dock. Reopen it for the PIN; press S or use windock stop to stop the host. Apps opened from the phone stay open.
  • Tailscale addresses: connect Android using your PC’s numeric Tailscale address when both devices are on your tailnet. Automatic discovery remains local-network only.

Update both the host and Android companion for the complete bundle. Clipboard sharing is text only; images and files are not included. Linux remains a Hyprland preview.

Built on the saved-PC contribution by Foxtrot Kreators.

EARLIER RELEASES

0.2.4-preview.1 October 25, 2026Less work for your phone. Safer pairing for your PC.

+
  • Performance mode reduces blur, shadows and touch effects, with instant paging. Choose Auto, On or Off in Appearance.
  • The updated Android companion can enable reduced effects automatically on low-RAM phones. Your manual choice is remembered.
  • Six-digit PINs, stronger attempt limits and DNS rebinding protection secure pairing and both live connections.
  • Clear wait messages and Allow pairing now in the Windows tray help recover from a pairing pause.
  • Update both apps. Android 0.2.2/0.2.3 updates in place; reopen and pair with the new PIN.

Thanks to ojaseminem for the security contribution and waly-git for the older-phone report.

0.2.3-preview.1 October 20, 2026Your Linux desktop. Within reach.

+
  • WinDock now runs on Hyprland Linux desktops, with apps, media, capture, the touchpad, phone keyboard and signatures.
  • Prebuilt Arch / Omarchy and Debian / Ubuntu packages bring their own runtime. Nothing to compile.
  • Touchpad, signature and capture messages fit every PC, and the touchpad no longer offers a Reconnect that can only fail again.
  • On Linux, apps you hid stay out of the app picker.
  • A website that’s easier to read and tap on phones, now with Linux downloads.
  • Update the PC app, then reconnect your phone. Android 0.2.2-preview.1 needs no update.

0.2.2-preview.1 October 19, 2026Plug in. Let your screen rest.

+
  • Connect over USB tethering with a data cable and your usual pairing PIN. No USB debugging needed.
  • Clear Wi-Fi and USB choices remember their own PC addresses. Restart without USB to return to Wi-Fi.
  • Choose idle dimming, brightness and screen rest in This phone, also available from Appearance in the dock.
  • Timed rest goes black and lets Android sleep. Your phone’s screen timeout may add a delay.
  • Update both apps. Uninstall previous public Android previews first because the signing key has changed; your PC dock settings stay.

Thanks to waly-git for suggesting USB and screen protection.
